# Getting Started with RetellDB

Welcome! This guide walks you through setting up your first RetellDB workspace so your support agents can answer questions from your documentation automatically.

## Step 1 — Create Your Workspace

1. Log in to the RetellDB dashboard at [app.retelldb.io](https://app.retelldb.io).
2. Click **New Workspace** in the top-right corner.
3. Enter a name (e.g., "ACME Support Docs") and click **Create**.

Your workspace is ready. Each workspace has two knowledge graphs automatically created: one for user-facing content and one for developer documentation.

## Step 2 — Upload Your Docs

1. Inside your workspace, click **Import Documents**.
2. Drag in Markdown files, or paste a Notion page URL.
3. Select the audience: **User Docs** or **Developer Docs**.
4. Click **Import** and wait a few seconds while RetellDB indexes everything.

## Step 3 — Test a Query

1. Click **Playground** in the sidebar.
2. Type a question your customers might ask, e.g., _"How do I reset my password?"_
3. RetellDB returns the most relevant sections from your documents, ranked by relevance.

## What's Next?

- Connect RetellDB to your live chat tool via the [Webhook Integration guide](#).
- Invite teammates under **Settings → Team Members**.
- Monitor retrieval accuracy in the **Analytics** tab.
