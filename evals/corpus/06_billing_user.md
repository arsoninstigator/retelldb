# Managing Your Subscription

This guide explains how to view, change, or cancel your RetellDB subscription from the dashboard.

## Viewing Your Current Plan

Navigate to **Settings → Billing** to see:

- Your current plan (Free, Starter, or Pro).
- The number of documents and query credits used this month.
- Your next invoice date and estimated amount.

## Upgrading Your Plan

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Upgrade Plan**.
2. Select the plan that fits your usage.
3. Enter your payment details — RetellDB accepts all major credit cards and invoice billing for annual plans.
4. Click **Confirm Upgrade**. Your new limits apply immediately.

## Cancelling Your Subscription

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription**.
2. Confirm your decision in the prompt.

Your workspace remains active until the end of the current billing period. After that, it reverts to the Free tier (100 documents, 500 queries/month). Your data is never deleted automatically.

## Downloading Invoices

All invoices are available under **Settings → Billing → Invoice History**. Click **Download PDF** next to any invoice.

## Payment Issues

If a payment fails, RetellDB will retry twice over 7 days and send email reminders. If you need help, contact **billing@retelldb.io**.
